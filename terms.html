There is no matchmaking, no dating, no relationship, and no expectation that you will find one. If you came for love, you're in the wrong place; if you came to be entertained, welcome.

3. Privacy & tracking

We do not track you. There are no user accounts, no logins, no profiles about you, and no database of your activity. Your swipes, likes, matches, and chats are generated and discarded on your own device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

The one exception is basic web analytics — the ordinary, aggregate hosting metrics used solely to keep the site online and scale it (e.g., how many visits a page gets, roughly where traffic comes from, error rates). This data is not tied to your identity, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and is not used to build a profile of you. It exists only so we know whether to buy a bigger server. That's it.

No cookies are required to use Unhinged. No personal data is collected, stored, or shared, because we designed the whole thing not to need any.

Be as unhinged as you like inside the app; be a normal person outside it.
